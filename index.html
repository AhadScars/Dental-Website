About the centre

Specialist care, family dentistry, one clinic.

Dr. Tasveer Fatima founded Elegancia Dental to bring implant and maxillofacial surgery together with everyday smile care. The main centre sits on Picnic Spot Road, opposite BPF in Khurram Nagar — a women-owned, LGBTQ+ friendly clinic.

Patients come for painless root canals, computer-guided implants, wisdom-tooth surgery, braces, and complete family dentistry. A second centre also serves Bakshi Ka Talab.

First visit

What happens when you come in.

A first appointment is unhurried. We listen, examine, and give you a clear plan before any treatment starts.

  1. 1. Listen and examineWe take your history, check teeth, gums, and bite, and use digital x-rays only when they help the plan.
  2. 2. Explain in plain languageYou leave knowing what is urgent, what can wait, and what each option costs in time and money.
  3. 3. Treat or book the next stepSimple care can often start the same day. Surgery and implants are planned, never rushed.
BringAny recent x-rays, a list of medicines, and a note of what hurts or worries you.
TimeA new patient visit is usually 30–45 minutes. Arrive a few minutes early.
PaymentPay at the desk after the visit. Treatment fees are confirmed before we start.
EmergencyToothache, swelling, or a broken tooth — call 072340 01111 for the next open chair.
